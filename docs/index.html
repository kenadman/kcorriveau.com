My View on AI

“We spent centuries building tools that extend the arm: the hammer, the car, the elevator. AI is the first tool that extends the mind, and that changes who should lead technology.”

  • AI Belongs With the CIO

    “The Chief AI Officer is this cycle’s Chief Digital Officer. AI belongs with the CIO, and the CIO needs to keep their fingers on the keyboard.”

  • Adoption Is a People Problem

    “AI rarely fails in the lab. It fails in the gap between what the C-suite believes and what the floor actually does.”

  • People Over Machine

    “The smart plan moves people toward the work a machine cannot do, not out the door.”
